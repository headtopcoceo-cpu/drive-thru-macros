      How the numbers were built

      • Nutrition comes from each chain's own Canadian data, pulled October 23, 2026: McDonald's nutrition calculator, Wendy's and Burger King ordering data, Harvey's nutrition page and A&W's menu data.
      • No sauce. McDonald's, Wendy's and Burger King publish ingredient-level numbers, so their no-sauce versions take the sauces off using the chain's own figures. A&W doesn't, so its no-sauce numbers are estimates (marked). Harvey's burgers are listed as bun and patty with toppings your choice.
      • Prices are before tax. Wendy's, Harvey's and Burger King come from their online ordering at Niagara Falls stores. McDonald's and A&W only show prices in their apps, so theirs are approximate, from third-party menu guides.
      • Cheat-meal score ranks your order's protein per 100 calories against every one-item order of a burger, chicken or fish main at these chains (no sauce, alone or with fries). Meals you logged keep the score they had when you logged them.
      • Combo score (Ideas) runs 0–100: 40% leanness (protein per 100 cal, where 3 g scores 0 and the 10 g golden ratio scores 100), 40% value (protein per dollar, where 2 g scores 0 and 7 g scores 100) and 20% protein (how close the meal gets to a third of your daily protein target). A meal from two restaurants loses 6 points for the extra stop, so it only ranks higher when it's clearly better.
      • Deals are refreshed weekly from the chains' own Ontario ordering sites. App coupons are personal, so add yours under Deals.
